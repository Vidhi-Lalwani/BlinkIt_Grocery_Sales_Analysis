# Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis: Exploratory Data Analysis Using Python

## 1. Project Overview

This project explores grocery sales data to identify patterns across product categories, outlet types, location tiers, and product characteristics.

The objective is to understand how recorded sales vary across product and outlet characteristics through exploratory data analysis (EDA).

## 2. Objectives

- Identify product categories with the highest total sales.
- Compare sales across outlet types and location tiers.
- Examine sales by product fat content.
- Explore relationships between item MRP, product visibility, outlet age, and sales.
- Investigate sales patterns across outlet sizes and product categories.

## 3. Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## 4. Dataset

The dataset contains 8,523 rows and 12 columns covering product characteristics, outlet information, and sales.

**Dataset source:** Tableau BlinkIT Grocery Project U16955293080 (4) - Tableau BlinkIT Grocery Project.csv


## 5. Data Cleaning and Preparation

The notebook includes:

- Inspection of dataset structure and data types.
- Checks for missing values and duplicate records.
- Treatment of missing item weights and outlet sizes.
- Standardization of inconsistent fat-content labels.
- Calculation of outlet age from establishment year.

The cleaning decisions are documented in the notebook.

## 6. Analysis Performed

1. Key performance indicator calculations.
2. Product category sales analysis.
3. Fat-content sales comparison.
4. Outlet type and location analysis.
5. MRP and sales analysis.
6. Product visibility and sales analysis.
7. Outlet age and sales analysis.
8. Additional comparisons involving outlet size and product categories.

## 7. Key Findings

- Fruits and Vegetables recorded the highest total sales among the product categories.
- Low-fat products recorded higher total sales than regular products.
- Supermarket Type1 recorded the highest total sales among outlet types.
- Recorded sales varied across outlet location tiers.
- Relationships between MRP, visibility, outlet age, and sales were explored using visualizations and correlation analysis.

These findings describe patterns in the available dataset. They do not establish that a particular product or outlet characteristic causes higher sales.

## 8. Limitations

- This is a descriptive analysis, not a predictive sales model.
- Correlation does not establish causation
